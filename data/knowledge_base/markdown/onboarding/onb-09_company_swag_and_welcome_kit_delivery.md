# Company Swag and Welcome Kit Delivery

- **Document ID**: `ONB-09`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: New Hire Experience
- **Keywords**: swag store, welcome kit, company t-shirt, backpack, swag points

## Policy & Procedure Details
During your first week, you will receive an invitation email from the NovaTech Swag Portal (swag.novatech.internal) containing 1,500 Swag Points credited to your account. You can select your personalized welcome pack including NovaTech branded apparel (hoodie or polo), insulated water bottle, North Face laptop backpack, and notebook. Items are shipped directly to your home address free of charge within 10 business days.
