# New Hire Benefits Enrollment 30-Day Window

- **Document ID**: `ONB-06`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: Health & Wellness
- **Keywords**: benefits enrollment deadline, new hire benefits, choose health plan, 30 day enrollment

## Policy & Procedure Details
New hires have exactly 30 calendar days from their start date to select and submit their health, dental, vision, life, and disability insurance elections in Workday. Benefits coverage is retroactive to your official hire date. If you fail to submit elections within the 30-day window, you will automatically be enrolled in the default Employee-Only Basic Health Plan, and you will be unable to add dependents or change coverage until the next annual Open Enrollment in November, unless you experience a Qualifying Life Event.
