# New Hire Onboarding Buddy Program

- **Document ID**: `ONB-04`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: New Hire Experience
- **Keywords**: buddy program, onboarding buddy, peer mentor, new hire buddy

## Policy & Procedure Details
Every new hire is assigned a designated 'NovaTech Buddy' from their peer team for the first 90 days. Your Buddy is a peer colleague who helps you navigate company culture, unblock development environment hurdles, understand team rituals, and introduce you to cross-functional partners. Buddies meet with you daily during Week 1, twice weekly during Weeks 2-4, and bi-weekly thereafter. Your buddy is not your evaluator, so feel free to ask them any practical or informal questions.
