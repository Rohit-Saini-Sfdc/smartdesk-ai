# Mandatory Compliance Training Requirements and Deadlines

- **Document ID**: `ONB-05`
- **Domain**: Onboarding

## Policy & Procedure Details
All new employees must complete mandatory compliance learning modules within their first 14 calendar days via the NovaTech Learning Portal (https://learn.novatech.internal). Required modules include: 1. Information Security & Phishing Awareness (45 mins); 2. Anti-Bribery, FCPA & Ethical Business Conduct (30 mins); 3. Preventing Harassment & Discrimination (POSH) (60 mins); 4. Data Privacy & GDPR/CCPA Fundamentals (30 mins). Failure to complete mandatory training by Day 14 results in temporary suspension of internal production code and database access.
