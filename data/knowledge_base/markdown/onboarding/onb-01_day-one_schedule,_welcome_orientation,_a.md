## Policy & Procedure Details
Welcome to NovaTech! Your first day begins at 9:30 AM local time. Remote hires join the virtual Zoom orientation link provided in your Welcome Email; in-office hires report to the reception desk on the 4th floor to receive their visitor pass and building badge. The day-one agenda consists of: 9:30 AM - Welcome & Company Overview with People Ops; 11:00 AM - IT Setup & Security Best Practices; 1:00 PM - Team Welcome Lunch with your Buddy; 2:30 PM - Benefits & Workplace Tools Q&A; 4:00 PM - 1:1 check-in with your direct manager.
