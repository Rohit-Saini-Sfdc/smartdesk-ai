# Direct Deposit and Bank Account Setup

- **Document ID**: `ONB-07`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: Compensation & Payroll
- **Keywords**: direct deposit setup, bank account, payroll setup, routing number

## Policy & Procedure Details
To ensure timely delivery of your first paycheck, enter your direct deposit bank details in the Workday portal under 'Pay > Payment Elections' no later than the 5th day of your first month. You may split direct deposit into up to 3 separate accounts (e.g., primary checking, savings). Required fields: Bank Name, 9-digit Routing Transit Number, and Account Number. Bank accounts must be in the legal employee's name. Any submissions after the monthly payroll cutoff will be issued via physical paper check mailed to your residential address.
