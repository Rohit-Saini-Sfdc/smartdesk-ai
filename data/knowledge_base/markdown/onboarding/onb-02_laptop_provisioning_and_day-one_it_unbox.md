## Policy & Procedure Details
For remote new hires, IT ships your laptop kit to arrive 2 to 3 business days before your official start date via FedEx Priority. The package contains: laptop, USB-C 96W charger, welcome setup booklet, and a temporary One-Time Password (OTP) envelope. On morning of Day One: 1. Power on the laptop and connect to home Wi-Fi. 2. Apple Automated Device Enrollment will display 'Remote Management - NovaTech Solutions'. 3. Enter the initial username and OTP provided in your personal welcome email to complete first-time configuration.
