# Probation Period Duration and Confirmation Review

- **Document ID**: `ONB-08`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: Performance & Career
- **Keywords**: probation period, probation review, confirmation, 90 day review

## Policy & Procedure Details
All new regular full-time employees undergo a standard 90-day probationary period. During this period, your manager will conduct regular 30, 60, and 90-day progress check-ins against onboarding 30-60-90 day deliverables. At the 85-day mark, People Ops triggers the formal Probation Confirmation form in Workday. Upon mutual manager and HR approval, you will receive a formal confirmation letter. In rare cases where onboarding milestones require further evaluation, probation may be extended for up to an additional 30 days.
