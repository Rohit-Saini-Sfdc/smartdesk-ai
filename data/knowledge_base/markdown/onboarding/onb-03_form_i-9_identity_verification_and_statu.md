# Form I-9 Identity Verification and Statutory Compliance

- **Document ID**: `ONB-03`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: Compliance & Documentation
- **Keywords**: i-9 verification, identity verification, passport verification, employment eligibility

## Policy & Procedure Details
Federal and statutory regulations require all new hires to complete Form I-9 (Employment Eligibility Verification) within 3 business days of their start date: Section 1 must be completed electronically in your Workday onboarding portal on or before Day One. Section 2 requires in-person document inspection. Remote employees will receive an automated link from our authorized verification partner (Equifax / I-9 Anywhere) to schedule an appointment at an authorized local center or complete verification with a designated remote notary.
