# Essential Slack Channels for New Hires

- **Document ID**: `ONB-10`
- **Domain**: Onboarding
- **Category**: Communication & Channels
- **Keywords**: slack channels, important channels, where to ask questions, welcome channel

## Policy & Procedure Details
Here are the essential Slack channels every new hire should join during their first week: 1. #welcome-novatech (say hello and introduce yourself to the company); 2. #ask-it (quick IT help and outage notices); 3. #ask-hr (benefits, leaves, policy clarifications); 4. #all-hands (announcements and monthly town hall links); 5. #shoutouts (celebrating teammate achievements); 6. Your specific departmental channel (e.g., #eng-core, #sales-enablement, #product-announcements). Join social and affinity ERG channels via the Slack channel browser.
