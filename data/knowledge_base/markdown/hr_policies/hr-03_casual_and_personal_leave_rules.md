# Casual and Personal Leave Rules

- **Document ID**: `HR-03`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Time Off & Leave
- **Keywords**: casual leave, personal leave, emergency leave, short notice leave

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech provides 6 days of Casual / Personal Leave per calendar year. Casual leave is designed for unforeseen personal events, urgent family obligations, or religious and cultural observances not covered by public company holidays. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive days at a time and cannot be combined with annual PTO without prior written approval from your People Partner. Unused casual leaves lapse on December 31st each year.
