# Educational Assistance and Professional Certification Reimbursement

- **Document ID**: `HR-13`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Learning & Development
- **Keywords**: education assistance, tuition reimbursement, certification reimbursement, learning stipend, udemy

## Policy & Procedure Details
To foster lifelong learning, full-time employees with at least 6 months of continuous tenure are eligible for up to $2,000 per calendar year in tuition and certification assistance. Covered items include industry certifications (AWS, GCP, PMP, CISSP), accredited university courses, and professional conference admissions relevant to your current role or defined career path. Employees must receive manager pre-approval prior to course commencement and achieve a passing score ('B' or higher, or verified certificate of completion) to submit for Concur reimbursement.
