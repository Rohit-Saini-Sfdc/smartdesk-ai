# Sick and Medical Leave Policy and Medical Certificate Guidelines

- **Document ID**: `HR-02`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Time Off & Leave
- **Keywords**: sick leave, medical leave, doctor note, medical certificate, extended sick

## Policy & Procedure Details
Employees receive 10 paid Sick and Medical Leave days per year, credited upfront on January 1st (or prorated for new hires). Sick leave is intended for personal illness, medical appointments, mental wellness recovery, or caring for an immediate sick family member. If taking 3 or more consecutive sick days, employees must provide a signed medical certificate from a licensed healthcare professional upon returning to work. Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following calendar year and is non-encashable upon resignation.
