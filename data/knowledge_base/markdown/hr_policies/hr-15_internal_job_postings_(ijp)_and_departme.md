# Internal Job Postings (IJP) and Department Transfer Policy

- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Performance & Career
- **Keywords**: internal transfer, ijp, switch teams, internal job application, career mobility

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech promotes internal mobility and career development. Eligibility criteria: 1. Employees must complete a minimum of 12 months in their current role with a rating of 'Consistently Meets Expectations' or above and no active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). 2. Browse open internal openings on the Greenhouse Internal Careers board. 3. You may apply and complete an exploratory interview before formally notifying your current manager; once you advance to the final interview stage, your current manager must be informed. Standard transition period between teams is 3 to 4 weeks upon acceptance of the internal offer.
