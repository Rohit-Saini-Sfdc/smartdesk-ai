# Health, Vision, and Dental Insurance and Dependent Coverage

- **Document ID**: `HR-12`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Health & Wellness
- **Keywords**: health insurance, dental insurance, vision coverage, add dependents, medical benefits

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech covers 100% of employee premium contributions and 80% of eligible dependent premiums for comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance through Blue Cross / UnitedHealthcare. Eligible dependents include spouses, domestic partners, and biological/adopted children up to age 26. Annual open enrollment takes place November 1st - November 20th. Outside of open enrollment, changes can only be made within 30 days of a Qualifying Life Event (QLE) such as marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of existing coverage by a spouse.
