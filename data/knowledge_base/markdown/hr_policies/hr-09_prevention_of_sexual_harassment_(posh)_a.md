## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech maintains a zero-tolerance policy against discrimination, harassment, sexual harassment, and retaliation of any kind. Any unwelcome verbal, non-verbal, physical, or digital conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment is strictly prohibited. Complaints can be filed directly with the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) at posh@novatech.com or through your HR Business Partner. All investigations are conducted with strict confidentiality within 30 calendar days. Retaliation against any individual reporting harassment results in immediate termination of employment.
