# Bereavement and Compassionate Leave

- **Document ID**: `HR-05`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Time Off & Leave
- **Keywords**: bereavement leave, funeral leave, compassionate leave, death in family

## Policy & Procedure Details
In the unfortunate event of the death of a family member, NovaTech provides paid compassionate leave: 1. Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling, legal guardian): Up to 5 consecutive business days of fully paid leave. 2. Extended family (grandparent, grandchild, in-laws): Up to 3 consecutive business days of fully paid leave. Additional unpaid leave or PTO may be requested if international travel is necessary. To request bereavement leave, notify your manager and People Partner via email.
