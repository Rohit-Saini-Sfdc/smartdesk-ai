# Parental Leave Policy (Maternity, Paternity, and Adoption)

- **Document ID**: `HR-04`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Family & Parental Support
- **Keywords**: parental leave, maternity leave, paternity leave, adoption leave, baby bonding

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech is committed to supporting expanding families with inclusive parental leave benefits: 1. Primary Caregiver (Maternity): 26 weeks of 100% paid leave for birthing mothers, which can begin up to 4 weeks prior to the expected delivery date. 2. Secondary Caregiver (Paternity / Partner): 12 weeks of 100% paid leave for fathers or non-birthing partners, which can be taken continuously or in 2-week increments within the first 12 months of the child's birth. 3. Adoption / Surrogacy: 16 weeks of 100% paid leave for primary adoptive parents. Benefits and stock vesting continue uninterrupted throughout all parental leave periods.
