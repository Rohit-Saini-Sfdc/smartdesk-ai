# Annual Performance Review Cycle and Promotion Process

- **Document ID**: `HR-10`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Performance & Career
- **Keywords**: performance review, appraisal cycle, promotions, peer feedback, performance ratings

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech operates a biannual performance enablement rhythm: 1. Mid-Year Check-in (July): Informal progress review focused on OKRs, career growth, and course correction; no formal numerical ratings. 2. Annual Review (December - January): Formal evaluation consisting of self-assessment, upward/peer feedback (minimum 3 peer reviewers), and manager evaluation. Rating scale: Exceeds Expectations (EE), Consistently Meets Expectations (CME), Needs Development (ND). Merit salary increases and promotions take effect annually on April 1st.
