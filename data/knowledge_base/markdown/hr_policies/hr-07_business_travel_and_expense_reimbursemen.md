# Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Limits

- **Document ID**: `HR-07`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Travel & Expenses
- **Keywords**: expense reimbursement, business travel, per diem, concur expenses, meal limit

## Policy & Procedure Details
All legitimate business expenses must be submitted via SAP Concur within 30 days of incurring the expense. Guidelines: 1. Meals: Daily per diem cap is $85 per day during business travel (Breakfast $20, Lunch $25, Dinner $40). Itemized receipts showing items purchased are mandatory for all expenses above $25; credit card charge slips alone are not accepted. 2. Air Travel: Economy class for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy or Business Class is eligible for non-stop international flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval. Alcohol is only reimbursable during authorized client-entertainment dinners.
