## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech partners with Modern Health to provide confidential Employee Assistance Program (EAP) services at zero cost to employees and their immediate household members. Benefits include 10 free one-on-one sessions per year with licensed clinical therapists or certified life coaches, 24/7 crisis helpline support, and specialized financial and legal consultation. EAP consultations are 100% confidential; no personal data or session details are ever shared with NovaTech HR or management. Access via https://my.modernhealth.com.
