# Employee Referral Bonus Program and Payout Schedule

- **Document ID**: `HR-11`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Talent & Recruiting
- **Keywords**: referral bonus, refer a candidate, referral payout, hiring referral

## Policy & Procedure Details
We encourage employees to refer top talent to NovaTech. Referral rewards: Standard Roles: $2,500; Senior / Leadership / Hard-to-Fill Roles: $5,000. Rules: 1. Candidate must be submitted through the Greenhouse recruiting portal before applying independently. 2. Payout occurs in 2 equal installments: 50% on the candidate's 30th calendar day of employment, and the remaining 50% upon successful completion of their 90-day probationary period. Both the referrer and referred employee must be active employees at the time of payout.
