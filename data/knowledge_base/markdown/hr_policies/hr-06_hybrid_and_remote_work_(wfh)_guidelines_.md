# Hybrid and Remote Work (WFH) Guidelines and Core Collaboration Hours

- **Document ID**: `HR-06`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Workplace Flexibility
- **Keywords**: wfh policy, hybrid work, work from home, remote work rules, core hours

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech operates on a flexible hybrid work model: 1. Hybrid Roles: Employees assigned to a regional hub are expected in the office 2 designated days per week (typically Tuesday and Thursday) for collaborative meetings. 2. Remote Roles: Designated remote employees work from their approved home residence within the hiring country. 3. Core Collaboration Hours: All employees must be accessible on Slack and Zoom during core team hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local regional time zone. Working from outside your country of employment is limited to 30 days per calendar year due to international corporate tax compliance and requires prior HR and Legal approval.
