# Code of Business Conduct and Conflict of Interest

- **Document ID**: `HR-08`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Ethics & Conduct
- **Keywords**: code of conduct, conflict of interest, outside employment, moonlighting, gifts policy

## Policy & Procedure Details
The NovaTech Code of Conduct outlines our commitment to integrity, compliance, and ethical operations. Key points: 1. Conflict of Interest & Moonlighting: Employees cannot engage in secondary employment, consulting, or advisory roles with competitors or vendors. Any external board positions or freelance activities must receive written pre-clearance from the Legal & Compliance Office. 2. Gifts and Hospitality: Employees may not accept gifts, entertainment, or gratuities valued over $75 from any client, partner, or supplier. Report suspected ethical breaches to ethics@novatech.com or the confidential whistleblower hotline.
