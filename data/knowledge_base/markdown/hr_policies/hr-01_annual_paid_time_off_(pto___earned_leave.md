# Annual Paid Time Off (PTO / Earned Leave) and Carry-Forward

- **Document ID**: `HR-01`
- **Domain**: HR Policies
- **Category**: Time Off & Leave
- **Keywords**: pto, earned leave, vacation days, carry forward pto, pto accrual

## Policy & Procedure Details
Full-time regular employees accrue 18 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any additional accrued unused PTO days beyond the 5-day cap expire on March 31st of the new year without financial encashment, unless mandated otherwise by local statutory laws. PTO requests of 3 consecutive days or more must be submitted in the HR Workday Portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by your manager.
