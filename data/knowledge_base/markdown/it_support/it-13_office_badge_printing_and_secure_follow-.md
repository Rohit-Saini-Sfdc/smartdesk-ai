## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech uses PaperCut Follow-Me secure printing across all regional offices. To print: 1. On your corporate laptop, print to the queue named 'NovaTech-Global-Print'. 2. Walk to any multi-function network printer in the office. 3. Tap your physical employee badge on the RFID card reader to unlock the machine and release your print jobs. Print jobs remain in the encrypted queue for 4 hours; unprinted jobs are automatically deleted for confidentiality.
