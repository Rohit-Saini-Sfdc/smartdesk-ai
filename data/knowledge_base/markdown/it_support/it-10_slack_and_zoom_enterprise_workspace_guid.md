# Slack and Zoom Enterprise Workspace Guidelines

- **Document ID**: `IT-10`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Collaboration Tools
- **Keywords**: slack guidelines, zoom enterprise, slack channels, zoom recording

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech uses Slack Enterprise Grid and Zoom Business for company communications. Guidelines: 1. Slack Channels: Use public channels (#proj-, #team-, #help-) by default to foster transparency. Private channels should be reserved for sensitive personnel, legal, or financial matters. Slack messages are retained for 1 year per corporate archiving policies. 2. Zoom: All employees have a Pro license supporting meetings up to 300 participants. Cloud recordings must be stored in Zoom Cloud and are auto-deleted after 90 days. External guests must remain in the waiting room until admitted by the host.
