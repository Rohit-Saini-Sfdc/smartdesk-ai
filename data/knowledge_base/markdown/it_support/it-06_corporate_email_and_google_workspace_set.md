## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech uses Google Workspace Enterprise for corporate email and calendar. To configure work email on your mobile phone: 1. Download the official Google Gmail and Google Calendar apps from the iOS App Store or Google Play Store. 2. Choose 'Add Account', enter your @novatech.com email, and complete Okta SSO + Duo MFA. 3. Mobile Device Management (MDM): You will be prompted to install the Google Device Policy profile (iOS) or set up an Android Work Profile. Personal data and corporate data remain completely isolated. Company security policy enforces an alphanumeric lock screen PIN (minimum 6 digits) or biometric FaceID/fingerprint.
