## Policy & Procedure Details
In all NovaTech physical offices, two primary wireless networks are broadcast: 1. 'NovaTech-Corporate': Dedicated to company-managed laptops with installed 802.1X certificates. Connect by selecting WPA3-Enterprise and authenticating with your corporate username and password. Do not connect personal smartphones or smart devices to this network. 2. 'NovaTech-Guest': Intended for visitors, contractors, and personal mobile devices. Connect to 'NovaTech-Guest', open a browser to view the captive portal, enter your email and host employee name, and accept the acceptable use terms. Guest sessions expire every 24 hours.
