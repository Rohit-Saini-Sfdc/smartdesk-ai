# Returning IT Equipment upon Departure or Replacement

- **Document ID**: `IT-15`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Hardware & Equipment
- **Keywords**: return laptop, offboarding equipment, it return label, send back laptop

## Policy & Procedure Details
Upon departure from NovaTech or receipt of a replacement laptop, all corporate equipment (laptop, power adapter, company-issued monitors or phones) must be returned to IT within 10 business days. IT Logistics emails a prepaid FedEx/UPS return label with a custom-padded box to the employee's home address. Prior to shipping, employees should sign out of personal Apple IDs or browser profiles. Do not attempt a manual factory reset, as the IT depot performs automated NIST-compliant cryptographic data sanitization.
