# Remote Work IT Equipment Stipend and Ordering

- **Document ID**: `IT-12`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Hardware & Equipment
- **Keywords**: home office stipend, wfh equipment, monitor allowance, ergonomic accessories

## Policy & Procedure Details
Full-time remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time Home Office IT Stipend of $750 (or local currency equivalent) upon hire, managed via the NovaTech IT Equipment Store at https://procurement.novatech.internal. Eligible items include external 4K monitors, USB-C docking stations, ergonomic keyboards, mice, webcams, and noise-canceling headsets. Items ordered through the company portal are direct-shipped to your residential address and billed directly to the IT budget without requiring out-of-pocket expense reimbursement.
