# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Setup and Device Transfer

- **Domain**: IT Support

## Policy & Procedure Details
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory for all NovaTech accounts using Duo Security. When logging in, approve the Duo Mobile push notification or enter the 6-digit TOTP code. If you get a new mobile device or phone number: 1. If you still have your old phone, log in to https://identity.novatech.internal/duo-settings, click 'Add a new device', and scan the QR code on your new phone. 2. If you lost your previous device or wiped it without registering a backup, you must complete a video identity verification call with the IT Service Desk to generate a temporary 24-hour bypass code.
