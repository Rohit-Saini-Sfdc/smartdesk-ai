# Hardware Refresh Cycle and Standard Laptop Specifications

- **Document ID**: `IT-07`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Hardware & Equipment
- **Keywords**: laptop refresh, hardware upgrade, macbook refresh, laptop replacement cycle

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech standard employee laptops operate on a 36-month (3-year) hardware refresh cycle. When your machine reaches 33 months of service, the IT Asset Management system automatically notifies you and your manager with an upgrade requisition link. Standard engineering profiles receive 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (M3/M4 Pro, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD) or Dell XPS 15 (i7/i9, 32GB RAM). Non-engineering roles receive 14-inch Apple MacBook Air (M3, 16GB RAM) or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Early replacements are only authorized in cases of verified catastrophic hardware failure or role changes requiring upgraded compute specifications.
