# Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR) and Account Unlock

- **Document ID**: `IT-01`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Account Access & Authentication
- **Keywords**: password reset, forgot password, unlock account, sspr, okta reset

## Policy & Procedure Details
All NovaTech employees can reset their corporate Active Directory / Okta password via the Self-Service Password Portal at https://passwords.novatech.internal. If your account is locked due to multiple failed attempts, visit the portal, click 'Unlock Account', and complete verification via your registered Duo MFA mobile app or SMS code. Passwords must be at least 14 characters, contain uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and a special character, and cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords. If you do not have access to your registered MFA device, contact the 24/7 IT Helpdesk at extension 4357 (HELP) or submit an IT ticket.
