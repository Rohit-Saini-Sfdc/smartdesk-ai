# Information Security Incident and Phishing Reporting

- **Document ID**: `IT-08`
- **Domain**: IT Support
- **Category**: Security & Compliance
- **Keywords**: phishing, suspicious email, report security, security incident, compromised

## Policy & Procedure Details
If you receive a suspicious email, SMS, or Slack message, or suspect your machine or credentials have been compromised: 1. Suspected Phishing: Click the 'FishAlarm / Report Phishing' button directly in the Gmail UI. This immediately alerts the Security Operations Center (SOC) and purges similar messages from other mailboxes. Do not click links or download attachments. 2. Potential Credential Compromise / Malware: Immediately disconnect your computer from Wi-Fi/Ethernet, lock the device, and alert the Security Incident Response Team (SIRT) via emergency hotline +1-800-555-SIRT or email security-alert@novatech.com.
