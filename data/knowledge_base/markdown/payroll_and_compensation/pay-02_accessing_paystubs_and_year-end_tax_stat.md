# Accessing Paystubs and Year-End Tax Statements (W-2 / Form 16)

- **Document ID**: `PAY-02`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Statements & Taxes
- **Keywords**: paystub, w-2, form 16, view payslip, tax statement download

## Policy & Procedure Details
Digital paystubs are available 2 business days prior to each payday via the Workday mobile app or web portal under 'Pay > View Payslips'. Payslips detail gross pay, statutory withholdings (federal/state taxes, social security), benefit deductions, and year-to-date totals. Annual tax statements (Form W-2 for US employees, Form 16 for India employees) are uploaded digitally no later than January 31st each year. Employees are notified via email when tax forms become available for download.
