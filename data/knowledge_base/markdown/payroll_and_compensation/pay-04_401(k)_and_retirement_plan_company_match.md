# 401(k) and Retirement Plan Company Match

- **Document ID**: `PAY-04`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Retirement & Savings
- **Keywords**: 401k, retirement plan, company match, fidelity 401k, pension

## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech offers a dollar-for-dollar 401(k) company match up to 5% of eligible base salary through Fidelity Investments. Employees are eligible to participate beginning on their first day of full-time employment. Company matching funds vest immediately (100% immediate vesting from Day 1). Employees can choose Traditional (pre-tax) or Roth (post-tax) contributions up to the statutory IRS annual limit. Manage your election and fund allocations directly on netbenefits.fidelity.com.
