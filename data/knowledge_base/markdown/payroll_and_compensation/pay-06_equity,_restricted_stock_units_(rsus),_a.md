## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech equity grants (RSUs or Stock Options) are administered via Carta. Standard vesting schedule: A 4-year vesting timeline with a 1-year cliff. Specifically: 25% of total grant vests exactly on the 1-year anniversary of your Vesting Start Date. Following the 1-year cliff, the remaining 75% vests in equal quarterly increments (6.25% per quarter) over the subsequent 36 months. Quarterly vesting dates are March 15, June 15, September 15, and December 15. View grant letters and exercised shares at https://carta.com.
