# Final Settlement and Severance Calculations

- **Document ID**: `PAY-09`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Separation & Settlement
- **Keywords**: final settlement, resignation pay, severance pay, encashment of leave on exit

## Policy & Procedure Details
Upon voluntary resignation or involuntary termination, an employee's final settlement (Full & Final) is processed in accordance with local labor statutes. The final settlement encompasses: prorated salary up to the final working day, encashment of eligible accrued unused PTO days (capped at statutory maximums), reimbursement of approved outstanding Concur expense claims, and any deduction for unreturned company assets. Final settlement disbursements are issued via standard direct deposit within 30 days of the last working day.
