# On-Call Compensation and Overtime Policy

- **Document ID**: `PAY-07`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Overtime & Special Pay
- **Keywords**: on-call pay, pagerduty compensation, overtime pay, incident response pay

## Policy & Procedure Details
Engineering and technical operations personnel assigned to primary production on-call rotation schedules receive dedicated on-call compensation: 1. Base On-Call Allowance: $350 per full week on primary on-call rotation ($175 for secondary backup), regardless of whether incidents occur. 2. Incident Response: If paged outside standard business hours for a P1/P0 production incident, engineers receive compensatory time off (Comp Off) equivalent to 1.5x time spent troubleshooting, rounded up to the nearest hour. Submit on-call tracking via PagerDuty monthly sync.
