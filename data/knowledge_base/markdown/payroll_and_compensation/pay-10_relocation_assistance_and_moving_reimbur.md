# Relocation Assistance and Moving Reimbursement Package

- **Document ID**: `PAY-10`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Relocation & Moving
- **Keywords**: relocation package, moving expenses, relocation reimbursement, relocation clawback

## Policy & Procedure Details
Employees hired into roles requiring physical relocation closer to a corporate hub may receive a lump-sum relocation package (typically $5,000 to $15,000 based on tier and household size), managed through our global mobility partner Cartus. Relocation packages cover moving van logistics, temporary housing for up to 30 days, and transit airfare. Relocation agreements include a standard 12-month clawback clause: if an employee voluntarily resigns within 12 months of relocation, a prorated portion of the relocation grant must be repaid to NovaTech.
