# Commuter Benefits and Transit Pass Subsidies

- **Document ID**: `PAY-08`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Commuter & Transit
- **Keywords**: commuter benefits, transit pass, parking subsidy, pre-tax transit

## Policy & Procedure Details
Employees working from a regional physical office are eligible for pre-tax commuter benefits administered through Edenred. You can set aside up to the IRS monthly pre-tax limit (currently $315/month) for qualified public transportation (subway, train, commuter bus) and $315/month for qualified parking. Additionally, NovaTech provides a direct $50/month company transit subsidy for employees utilizing green public transit instead of driving single-occupancy vehicles. Enroll by the 10th of the prior month via Edenred portal.
