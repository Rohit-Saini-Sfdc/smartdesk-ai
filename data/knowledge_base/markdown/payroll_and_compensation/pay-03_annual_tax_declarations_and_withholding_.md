# Annual Tax Declarations and Withholding Adjustments

- **Document ID**: `PAY-03`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Statements & Taxes
- **Keywords**: tax declaration, w-4, withholding tax, investment proofs, tax saving

## Policy & Procedure Details
Employees can update their tax withholding preferences (Form W-4 allowances or state withholding) anytime throughout the year in Workday under 'Pay > Withholding Elections'. For employees subject to investment proof verification, the declaration window opens on April 1st and final proof submission closes on January 15th of the tax year. Failure to upload valid investment proofs by January 15th results in standard automated tax deductions without provisional relief on remaining paychecks.
