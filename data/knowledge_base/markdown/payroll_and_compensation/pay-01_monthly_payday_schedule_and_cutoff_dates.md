## Policy & Procedure Details
NovaTech distributes salary payments on a semi-monthly schedule for US employees (15th and the last business day of each month) and on a monthly schedule (the 25th of each month) for international subsidiaries. If the scheduled payday falls on a weekend or public bank holiday, funds are credited on the immediate preceding business day. Payroll adjustments (bonus payouts, overtime, unpaid leave deductions) close on the 10th of the month for mid-month payroll and on the 20th for month-end payroll.
