# Annual Performance Bonus and Variable Incentive Criteria

- **Document ID**: `PAY-05`
- **Domain**: Payroll & Compensation
- **Category**: Incentives & Bonuses
- **Keywords**: annual bonus, variable pay, bonus payout date, company multiplier

## Policy & Procedure Details
Annual performance bonuses are determined by a combination of overall company performance (Corporate Multiplier: 0% to 150%) and individual performance evaluation ratings (Individual Multiplier: 0% to 130%). Target bonus percentages are defined in your employment contract (typically 10% to 25% of annual base salary). Bonus payouts are processed in the March 31st payroll cycle following the annual review. To be eligible for payout, an employee must have joined prior to October 1st and remain actively employed on the payout date.
